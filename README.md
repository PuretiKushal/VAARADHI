
# VAARADHI – Government Scheme Discovery & Eligibility Platform

**Bridging the gap between citizens and government welfare schemes.**

[![Live Demo](https://img.shields.io/badge/Live%20Demo-Visit%20VAARADHI-2ea44f?style=for-the-badge)](https://vaaradhi-project.vercel.app/)

## About the Project

VAARADHI is a web-based platform designed to make discovering and understanding Indian government welfare schemes easier.

Information about government schemes can often be scattered across different portals, making it difficult for citizens to identify schemes relevant to their circumstances. VAARADHI brings scheme discovery, eligibility checking, Aadhaar assistance, and service-centre location features together in one place.

The platform focuses on helping users explore available schemes based on their personal details and access useful guidance through a simple interface.

## Key Features

### 1. Government Scheme Eligibility Checker
- Enter details such as age, income, occupation, gender, marital status, and state.
- Check eligibility against a predefined collection of Central and State Government schemes.
- View eligible schemes and reasons for ineligibility.

### 2. Scheme Information
- Explore information about different government schemes.
- View scheme categories, applicable states, benefits, and eligibility details.
- Access relevant information through individual scheme detail views.

### 3. Aadhaar Assistance
- Access Aadhaar-related guidance and document information.
- Watch tutorials in six languages:
  - Telugu
  - Hindi
  - English
  - Tamil
  - Kannada
  - Malayalam

### 4. Office Locator
- Interactive map powered by OpenStreetMap and Leaflet.
- Browser-based location detection.
- Search for locations and explore mapped service-centre markers.

### 5. Vaaradhi AI – Chat Assistant
- Interact with a built-in chatbot for basic scheme-related queries.
- Find matching schemes using keywords related to scheme names, states, and occupations.
- Get guidance on using the platform and its features.

## Disclaimer

VAARADHI is an independent project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or officially associated with the Government of India or any State Government.

The scheme information and eligibility results are indicative and based on the data and conditions available in the platform. The office locator uses prototype mapped locations and should not be treated as a verified, real-time government office directory.

Users should verify scheme details, eligibility requirements, and application procedures through the relevant official government portals before making decisions.
